About Lauren

A smooth, smoky voice with a theatrical heart

Lauren Beck is a professional and versatile narrator who brings emotional depth and meticulous attention to every detail of a story. Her smooth, smoky, and engaging voice moves effortlessly between the swoon of a rom-com, the tension of a paranormal romance, the edge of a thriller, and the sweep of a fantasy.

Based in Louisiana, Lauren is a devoted mom of three, a rescuer of animals, and a regular at metalcore concerts. She runs on good coffee and witty conversation, and she pours that same warmth and wit into her characters, because the best narration doesn't just read a book, it lives inside it.
